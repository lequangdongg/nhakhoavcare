A bridge replaces a missing tooth by spanning crowns across the two teeth on either side.

## The real cost

Both supporting teeth have to be filed down, even when they are perfectly healthy. This is the biggest difference from an implant, which leaves the neighbouring teeth untouched.

## When a bridge still makes sense

When the neighbouring teeth already carry crowns or large fillings, preparing them costs little extra. When there is not enough bone for an implant and the patient does not want a graft, a bridge is the only fixed option.
