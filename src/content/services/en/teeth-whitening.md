Whitening uses a peroxide gel that passes through the enamel and breaks down the molecules causing the colour.

## Which stains respond

Staining from tea, coffee, tobacco and age usually responds well. Tetracycline staining from childhood antibiotics responds poorly and may need veneers instead.

## Crowns and fillings

Porcelain and composite do not change colour with bleaching gel. If you have a crown on a front tooth, your natural teeth will end up lighter than the crown, and the crown will need remaking to match.

## Sensitivity

Common in the first few days and it settles on its own.
