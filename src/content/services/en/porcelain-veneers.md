A veneer is a thin porcelain layer bonded to the outer face of a front tooth. It changes colour, shape, or closes a small gap.

## Does the tooth get filed down

Yes, but only slightly. A veneer touches the outer face only and leaves most of the natural tooth intact. This is the fundamental difference from a crown, which is prepared all the way around.

## When a veneer is the wrong choice

A tooth with a large filling, a root-treated tooth, or a tooth under heavy bite force will lift or crack a veneer. In those cases a crown lasts longer.
