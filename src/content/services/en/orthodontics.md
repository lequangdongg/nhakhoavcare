Orthodontics moves teeth into position using light continuous force, either through fixed braces or clear aligners.

## How long it takes

Most cases run from one to three years, depending on how much movement is needed and the patient age. Adult bone remodels more slowly, so adult treatment usually takes longer.

## Retainers

Once the braces come off, teeth tend to drift back. A retainer is required, not optional. Stopping retainer wear is the most common reason orthodontic results are lost a few years later.

## Braces or aligners

Aligners look better but depend entirely on the patient wearing them enough hours each day.
