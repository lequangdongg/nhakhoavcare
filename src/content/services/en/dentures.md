A denture replaces several missing teeth with an appliance the patient takes out and puts back in.

## Getting used to it

The first few weeks usually feel bulky, speech is not quite right and eating takes practice. This is normal and does not mean the denture was made badly.

## Why it loosens over time

Once teeth are gone, the jawbone shrinks year by year. A denture rests on that bone, so it gradually loosens and needs relining or replacing. Implants preserve bone better because chewing still sends force into it.
