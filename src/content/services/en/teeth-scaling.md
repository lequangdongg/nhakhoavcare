Tartar is plaque that has hardened. A toothbrush cannot remove it. It builds up around the neck of the tooth and under the gum margin.

## Why it has to come off

The surface of tartar is rough and holds bacteria against the gum, which causes inflammation. Long-standing inflammation leads to bone loss around the tooth, and lost bone does not grow back.

## Bleeding during the appointment

Healthy gums do not bleed. Bleeding means the gums were already inflamed, not that the cleaning caused it.

## How often

Usually every six months. Smokers and heavy tartar formers need it more often.
