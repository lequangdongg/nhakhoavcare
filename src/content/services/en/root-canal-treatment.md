Root canal treatment removes inflamed or dead pulp from inside the tooth, cleans the canal system and seals it.

## The pain stopped, do I still need it

Yes. Pain stopping usually means the pulp has died, not that the problem is gone. Bacteria keep growing and spread to the root tip, which forms an abscess.

## Does it hurt

The procedure is done under local anaesthetic. The uncomfortable part is usually the few days after, while the tissue around the root tip is still inflamed.

## Afterwards

The tooth becomes brittle and should be crowned to prevent a vertical split.
