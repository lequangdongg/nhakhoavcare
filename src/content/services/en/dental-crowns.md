A crown covers the whole part of the tooth above the gum, restoring its shape and chewing function.

## Why a root-treated tooth should be crowned

A tooth that has had its nerve removed loses its blood supply, becomes brittle, and tends to split vertically. A vertical split usually cannot be saved and the tooth has to come out. A crown is how that is avoided.

## Crown materials

Metal-backed porcelain is cheaper but can show a dark line at the gum over time. All-ceramic gives a more natural colour with no dark line. Zirconia is the strongest and is usually chosen for back teeth.
