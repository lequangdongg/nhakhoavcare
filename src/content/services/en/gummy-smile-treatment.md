A gummy smile is when a lot of gum shows when smiling. There are three main causes and the treatment differs completely.

## Gum covering too much of the tooth

The teeth are actually long enough but partly hidden. Gum contouring exposes the correct proportion of the tooth.

## Overgrowth of the upper jaw

Gum surgery will not fix this one. It needs orthognathic treatment.

## A short or high-pulling upper lip

This is treated at the lip, not the gum. Getting the diagnosis right is the most important step, because treating the wrong cause changes nothing.
