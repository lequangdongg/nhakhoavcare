Fluoride varnish is a coating painted onto tooth enamel. It hardens the enamel against the acid that bacteria produce.

## When to start

It can be applied as soon as the first tooth appears. The application is quick, painless and needs no anaesthetic.

## How often

Usually every six months. Children at higher risk of decay may need it more frequently.

## What it does not replace

Varnish does not replace brushing twice a day and it does not cancel out a high-sugar diet. It is an extra layer of protection, not the only one.
