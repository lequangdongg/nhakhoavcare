An implant is a titanium post placed into the jawbone to replace the root of a missing tooth. Once bone has grown onto the surface of the post, a crown is fitted on top.

Unlike a bridge, an implant does not require filing down the neighbouring teeth. Unlike a denture, it stays in place and does not come out at night.

## When it cannot be done straight away

If the jawbone has shrunk after years without a tooth, a bone graft comes first. Some systemic conditions and heavy smoking raise the failure rate. The dentist will tell you after reading the CT scan.

## How long it takes

Bone integration is the longest stage and it cannot be rushed. It usually takes several months. If someone promises the whole thing in a week, ask what exactly they are doing.
